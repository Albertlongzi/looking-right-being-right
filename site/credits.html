Reconstruction display

The scenes are saved outputs of this study, aligned to the reference coordinate frame and converted to browser geometry. Materials are approximated for display. The animation reveals final surfaces along the input camera path; it does not depict the agents’ actual reconstruction timing. The scan comparison uses the same view and a shared cutaway plane. Scan colors show height, not geometric error.

Benchmark

The score atlas combines 69 publicly released reconstruction configurations, rescored geometrically, with two video-only GPT-6.2 evaluations from our harness. Reconstruction setups differ. Entries retain source labels; intervals are 95% confidence intervals.
